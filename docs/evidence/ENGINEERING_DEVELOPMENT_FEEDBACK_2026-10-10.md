Tests may run together when their inputs are ready and writable checkout,
generation, environment, state, store, processes and ports are isolated.
Readers wait for the actual successful generation; a writer must not rebuild its
SDK/Runtime output while a runtime consumes it. Remote isolated CI can overlap a
local diagnostic. Source review, read-only audits and disjoint authors can overlap
heavy work. Data admission precedes use; fixed data and code precede training;
verified package/readiness precedes learned load. A queued job is no evidence.

Do not rerun a broad suite without a changed candidate, new failure/evidence,
uncovered risk or required gate. Test changes need their actual contract and
counterexample; preserve original failures. Expensive immutable fixture production
may be reused only through private exact copies with isolation proof. A smaller
development check never silently deletes required merge or qualification coverage.

## When to change the design or the investment

Escalate the specific boundary when correct participants cannot express the
required behavior, multiple layers reconstruct one high-risk fact, new real
mechanisms repeatedly need special exceptions, unrelated identities invalidate
otherwise compatible consumers, or measured operation/migration/test cost exceeds
the task's useful contribution. Compare a cleaner owner/contract, compatibility
boundary and narrower explicitly deferred scope against continuing patches.
Keep the necessary fair-player, immutable-source, unknown and cleanup guarantees.

Use one cheap faithful counterexample before another expensive cycle. A disproved
explanation is discarded; another failure of the same assumption is a reason to
review the abstraction, not accumulate independent workarounds. A measured job cap
is an adjustable resource policy, distinct from a product action deadline. Current
partitioning retains55 minutes; no universal duration is claimed.

## Current order and safe parallel work

1. Integrate reviewed owning repairs and freeze one candidate. Finish relevant
   local source/seam/exact-build/Host/artifact checks and independently verify the
   named prerequisite receipt.
2. Publish one current full CI run. Its four isolated leaves and aggregate proceed
   alongside owning install/cold-load and the already-authorized ONE Source-off
   target100 diagnostic. Root owns the local heavy/native slot and preserves the
   max200/known-stale8-total3-consecutive/15-minute bounds. A real problem ends it
   safely; no second diagnostic or automatic repair/restart follows.
3. In parallel, perform read-only producer/closure, stopped-archive/coverage and
   model/public-manifest/UI/Stop audits. Disjoint contract preparation is possible;
   source changes must not mutate the running candidate or silently alter future
   training identity.
4. After the diagnostic report, explicitly admit qualifying stopped originals
   with the reviewed matching producer relation; report actual N, exclusions and
   coverage. Historical six/eight labels and Source3/direct aliases are not padding.
5. Freeze the compatible train dataset and code, then run genuine new training,
   checkpoint/Model publication, export/registration/readiness and native load.
   Use the existing common recipe and application owners.
6. Run this learned Agent in a fresh game to qualified natural win/loss summary,
   with visible model identity, actual native access and confirmed Stop. Loop,
   budget, unsupported or unknown outcomes remain incomplete with attribution.
7. Complete applicable selected gates, whole-chain independent review and
   integration/handoff. Main release and full G2/V1 approval remain separate.

The first larger closure needs genuine new data/fit/loaded-model/continuous-game
evidence, not another trainer or all optional cloud/Human/pressure work beforehand.
Basic action/state-dependent behavior and progression must be observed; a loaded
weight digest, falling fit loss or legal/random actions alone do not establish
strategy quality. Report decision examples and comparison conditions, then use
observed failures to choose the next data/model/environment improvement.

There is no reliable completion date while the native outcome, eligible real N
and learned task behavior are unknown. The next feedback point is the bounded
diagnostic after its local prerequisites; its actual game phase lasts at most
15 minutes. Runtime results and later data/model acceptance belong in their
own evidence, rather than being backfilled into this source retrospective.
