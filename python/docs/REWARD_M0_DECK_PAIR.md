# Reward-M0 deck-context paired experiment

This is an offline engineering experiment. The retained d6 legacy compact
model and original reward loop remain unchanged. A TRAIN alias at sequences
408/414 in a Silent run has byte-identical legacy state, identical semantic
candidate multiset and opposite original Skip/Take targets; its original
Human-time deck Reads contain25/27cards. This establishes projection aliasing,
not the cause of the separate Defect loop or a proved gameplay correction.

New typed Source/InputSpec/Run/Checkpoint/Model identities describe two matched
arms. A retains exact legacy `project_public_snapshot(..., compact=True)` text.
B appends a versioned canonical unordered-multiset run-deck block. Both arms
retain the same explicit≤300 unique TRAIN occurrence IDs, original targets and
complete ordered C. Opaque card entity IDs are removed; card multiplicity,
upgrades, visible enchantments, descriptions and string-valued costs remain.
Missing optional nullable fields normalize to null under the owning VisibleCard
schema. No card order, missing contents, choice or history is inferred.

The cached public view supplies only locators and expected exact text. Selected
rows are independently joined through original allocation TRAIN membership,
verified archive/proof/H frame and its materialized Read evidence. The Read
blob is content only; its recorded envelope binds snapshot/runtime/environment,
Read opportunity, completeness and byte SHA. No successor or later frame may
repair missing B input. Such an occurrence is excluded from both arms with its
reason. Cache payload bytes are streamed through existing immutable Store
integrity checks, without reprojecting the full corpus or using dev/Gold inputs.

One fixed train-only tokenizer is fitted on the paired union of A/B state text.
Both arms use that tokenizer, the existing scratch M0 graph and matching
initialization/seed/update order/optimizer/configured limits. Both arms undergo
complete token/action-byte preflight; no truncation or candidate filtering.
They have distinct ordinary TrainingInput/Run/Checkpoint/Model artifacts, exact
source/arm identities and normal curation/use receipts. New fit is distinct
from resuming d6. Only CPU/MPS is supported; unavailable MPS fails without
fallback. Paid/provider execution is unsupported by this recipe.

The common LocalDatasetService and LocalTrainingService own preparation,
reservation, actual use, journals, explicit controls, child fences, wall/scratch
budgets, publication and result selection. Trusted recipe dispatch selects the
owning reward-M0 backend; existing structured and legacy identities retain
their meaning. Numerical model/optimizer/RNG/checkpoint helpers are reused.
Root freezes and reviews the actual cohort and cost/limits before any actual
fit or Model publication. Initial author validation uses synthetic fixtures.

Report paired training NLL/top1 and per-surface target distributions as internal
training diagnostics only, including collisions and eligibility loss. A408/414
equality/Bdifference is a representation check, not a required prediction
contradiction. Online Snapshot+Read adapter, export/registration and fresh
reward/game evaluation are separate follow-up gates; this offline packet
cannot claim natural summary, loop resolution or strategy quality.
